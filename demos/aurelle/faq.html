Shipping & delivery

Where do you ship?

We ship to all 50 US states. White-glove delivery is available in the contiguous US; Alaska and Hawaii orders ship insured ground. International orders are available on request through client care.

How much does shipping cost?

White-glove delivery is complimentary on orders over $750. Below that, insured ground shipping is a flat $29 per order.

When will my order arrive?

Pieces marked “Ready to ship” leave our New Jersey warehouse within 1–2 business days and arrive in 3–7 business days. Made-to-order pieces ship in 3–4 weeks; we’ll confirm an exact date by email.

What is white-glove delivery?

Our delivery partners schedule a two-hour window with you, bring the piece into the room of your choice, unpack it and take away all packaging. Installation is not included, but we’re happy to recommend a licensed electrician.

How do I track my order?

You’ll receive a tracking link by email and SMS as soon as your order ships. You can also check status any time from your account.

Returns & trial

What is the 30-night home trial?

Live with your lighting for up to 30 nights. If it isn’t right for your room, return it in its original packaging for a full refund to your original payment method.

How do I start a return?

Email client care with your order number, or start a return from your account. We’ll send a prepaid label, or arrange a collection for larger pieces.

Are any items final sale?

Custom sizes, custom finishes and pieces marked “Final sale” can’t be returned. Installed fixtures can be returned only if they are faulty.

My piece arrived damaged. What should I do?

We’re so sorry. Please photograph the piece and packaging and email us within 7 days. We’ll ship a replacement right away at no cost.

Products & installation

Are your fixtures UL listed?

Yes. Every Aurelle fixture is UL or ETL listed for use in the US and Canada, and most are rated for damp locations. Each product page lists its rating.

Can I use a dimmer?

All our fixtures are dimmable with an LED-compatible (trailing-edge) dimmer. Plug-in lamps include an in-line or touch dimmer where noted.

Are bulbs included?

Yes. We include warm 2700K LED bulbs chosen for each fixture, so the glow looks exactly as designed.

Do I need an electrician?

Pendants, chandeliers and hardwired sconces should be installed by a licensed electrician. They fit standard US junction boxes and come with all mounting hardware.

How do I choose the right size?

A good rule: a chandelier’s diameter should be about half to two-thirds the width of your table, hung 30–36" above it. For anything else, our stylists will size it for you in a free consultation.

Why does my alabaster look different from the photo?

Alabaster is a natural stone, so every piece has its own veining and tone. That variation is what makes each lamp one of a kind.

Care & warranty

How do I clean my lighting?

Turn off and let the fixture cool, then dust with a soft dry cloth. Avoid water and chemical cleaners on stone, plaster and unlacquered brass.

What does the warranty cover?

Our 5-year warranty covers electrical components and manufacturing defects. Natural changes such as brass patina or stone variation are not defects.

Do you offer trade pricing?

Yes. Interior designers, architects and hospitality professionals receive 15% trade pricing, a dedicated project manager and spec sheets on request. Apply here.

What is a design consultation?

A free 30-minute video call with one of our lighting stylists. Send photos and measurements, and we’ll recommend pieces, sizes and placement for your room.

Do you make custom pieces?

For trade and larger residential projects we can produce custom sizes, finishes and cord lengths. Lead times start at 6 weeks.
