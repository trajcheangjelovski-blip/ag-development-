About VELOCE

More than a showroom —
a curator of desire

VELOCE is a boutique showroom specialising in luxury and performance vehicles. Instead of a broad catalogue, we offer a carefully selected collection where every car must meet our strict standards for condition, history and authenticity.

Our team knows these cars down to the finest detail — from rare configurations to service quirks. We advise you honestly, without pressure, so you make a decision you'll be happy with for years.
