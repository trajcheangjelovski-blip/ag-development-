How we sell

No surprises after the handshake.

Buying a car at this level should be straightforward. This is what comes with every car we sell, whatever the price.

  1. 01

    Inspected before it’s listed

    Each car goes through a 150-point check at our partner workshop. You get the full report, with photos of the underside.

  2. 02

    A complete history file

    Service records, previous owners, import and customs papers and an independent mileage check, in one folder that stays with the car.

  3. 03

    Twelve months’ warranty

    Mechanical and electrical cover on every car, extendable to 36 months. Claims are handled by us, not a call centre.

  4. 04

    Registered, insured, delivered

    We take care of registration and insurance and can deliver on an enclosed transporter anywhere in North Macedonia and the neighbouring countries.

About

A small showroom, on purpose.

Veloce started in 2012 importing cars for private clients who knew exactly what they wanted. We still work that way. We keep around forty cars on the floor and we know each of them well: where it came from, who owned it and what has been done to it.

We would rather sell you the right car next month than the wrong one today. If something in stock isn’t right for you, we’ll say so, and we’ll find one that is.
